Lexora Prototype Demo Video Script
Approximate duration: 3 minutes

Suggested flow: Home -> Intake -> Review -> Insights -> Search -> Vault

[0:00 - 0:20]
On screen:
- Lexora logo and dashboard
- Quick cuts of scattered contracts across email, folders, and PDFs

Voiceover:
"Legal teams do not struggle because contracts are rare. They struggle because contracts are everywhere. Important agreements are spread across uploads, shared drives, inbox attachments, and disconnected files, which makes review slow, repetitive, and easy to miss."

[0:20 - 0:45]
On screen:
- Home page with metrics
- Highlight risk count, clause count, and review flow

Voiceover:
"That is the problem Lexora is solving. Our prototype turns fragmented legal documents into one review workflow. Instead of manually hunting for files, reading every page from scratch, and trying to spot risky language alone, the team gets a structured path from intake to insight."

[0:45 - 1:10]
On screen:
- Intake page
- Upload a contract
- Show connector cards for Manual Upload, Google Drive, and Gmail

Voiceover:
"We start in Intake. Lexora accepts contracts through manual upload and is designed to work across connected sources like Google Drive and Gmail. Once a file enters the system, the backend stores the original document, extracts the text, sends it to our machine learning pipeline, and prepares it for search and review."

[1:10 - 1:40]
On screen:
- Review page
- Open a tracked contract
- Expand clause review
- Show clause labels, risk badges, extracted values, and full clause text

Voiceover:
"After processing, Lexora breaks the contract into clause-level intelligence. Reviewers can immediately see the contract type, parties, key dates, and a risk summary. More importantly, each clause is classified and scored, so the legal team can focus first on high-risk areas like termination, payment exposure, or penalty language instead of losing time on low-priority sections."

[1:40 - 2:10]
On screen:
- Insights page
- Show high-risk clause insights
- Show current clause versus best precedent or comparison
- Highlight 'Why it is risky', 'Recommended change', and 'Get Report'

Voiceover:
"Lexora does more than flag risk. In the Insights workspace, it explains why a clause is risky, compares it with stronger reference language, and recommends what should change. That means the reviewer is not just told that something is wrong. They are given grounded reasoning, better drafting direction, and a faster path toward action."

[2:10 - 2:35]
On screen:
- Search page
- Ask a natural-language question
- Run typed search, then show Voice Ask
- Highlight answer, recommendations, and supporting matches

Voiceover:
"If the reviewer needs deeper context, semantic search makes the contract searchable in plain language. A user can ask, 'What makes this termination clause risky?' and Lexora returns an answer, suggested next steps, and the supporting clause matches behind that answer. This reduces manual scanning and helps legal teams retrieve useful precedent-style context much faster."

[2:35 - 2:50]
On screen:
- Vault page
- Search by file name
- Open original document preview
- Show Open and Download actions

Voiceover:
"And because legal review must stay grounded in the source, Lexora also keeps the original file accessible. In Vault, teams can find the stored document by name, open the native file, and verify every insight against the actual contract."

[2:50 - 3:00]
On screen:
- End montage of Intake, Review, Insights, Search, and Vault
- Close on Lexora branding

Voiceover:
"So the value of Lexora is simple: it transforms scattered contracts into a focused legal intelligence workflow, helping teams identify hidden risk earlier, review faster, and make better decisions with confidence."
